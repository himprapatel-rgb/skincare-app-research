## 2. Primary Revenue Streams

### 2.1 Freemium Model

| Feature | Free Tier | Premium Tier |
|---------|-----------|-------------|
| Skin scans per month | 3 | Unlimited |
| Analysis depth | Basic | Advanced + AI insights |
| Product recommendations | Top 3 | Full personalized list |
| Progress tracking | 30 days | Unlimited history |
| Virtual try-on | Limited | Full AR features |
| Export reports | No | Yes (PDF, share) |
| Ad-free experience | No | Yes |

### 2.2 Subscription Tiers

| Plan | Price (Monthly) | Price (Annual) | Target User |
|------|-----------------|----------------|-------------|
| Free | $0 | $0 | Casual users |
| Plus | $9.99 | $79.99 ($6.67/mo) | Regular users |
| Pro | $19.99 | $149.99 ($12.50/mo) | Skincare enthusiasts |
| Family | $29.99 | $239.99 ($20/mo) | Up to 5 profiles |

### 2.3 Affiliate & E-commerce

- **Product Affiliate Links:** 5-15% commission per sale
- **In-app Marketplace:** Direct product purchases (10-20% margin)
- **Brand Partnerships:** Featured placement fees ($5K-$50K/month)
- **Exclusive Discounts:** Partner brand promotions

## 3. Secondary Revenue Streams

### 3.1 B2B / Enterprise

| Offering | Price Range | Target |

| API Access | $500-$5,000/month | Developers, startups |

### 3.2 Data & Insights (Anonymized)

- **Market Research Reports:** Skin trend analytics for brands
- **Product Efficacy Data:** Before/after analysis for CPG companies
- **Regional Insights:** Geographic skin concern patterns
- **Ingredient Performance:** Anonymized outcome tracking

*All data is anonymized and aggregated. Users must opt-in.*
